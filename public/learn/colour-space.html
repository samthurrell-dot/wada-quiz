Where Wada’s Colours Live

Wada’s 159 colours plotted by hue, strength and lightness, and his 348 combinations measured for contrast. It shows which parts of the colour wheel he favoured, and how he put colours together.

The colour wheel

Angle is hue. Distance from the centre is strength (chroma): greys sit in the middle, the most intense colours at the rim. Bigger dots appear in more combinations.

The warm side is crowded.

The blue side stays soft. Part of this is the printing: cyan and blue inks cannot reach the strength of printed reds and yellows.

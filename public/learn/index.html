Sanzo Wada (1883–1967) was a Japanese painter and costume designer who spent much of his life studying colour. In the early 1930s he published a six-volume guide to putting colours together. It was republished in 2010 as A Dictionary of Color Combinations: 159 colours arranged into 348 combinations.

These pages look at where those colours and their names came from, and at the patterns behind the combinations.

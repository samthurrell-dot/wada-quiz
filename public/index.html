Wada you know about colour?

Ten colours from Sanzo Wada’s Dictionary of Color Combinations. Name each one. Stuck? Each colour has a cryptic crossword clue, and a second hint that shows where the definition is.

Pick a name your friends will recognise. No sign-up needed; this device remembers you.

How do you want to answer?
Which round?
Each colour: 6 points with no hints, 3 with the clue, 1 with both hints, 0 if wrong. Type mode doubles it.
How the cryptic clues work

Every clue has two parts. The definition, at the start or end, describes the colour. The rest is wordplay that builds the name: anagrams (look for words like twisted or badly), words joined end to end, hidden words, or sound-alikes. Colour words have fixed stand-ins: red is Communist or flushed, blue is down or sad, green is naive or envious, yellow is cowardly or chicken, pink is blushing or in fine fettle, purple is royal. After each answer you see how the clue works.

Each phone or computer counts as a separate player. To join them into one, copy your link below and open it on your other device. Its scores are then combined with this one’s, under one name. If both devices played the same day, the first round counts.
